# Privacy Policy — 7Cav Staff Toolkit

**Last Updated:** 16 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

The 7Cav Staff Toolkit is an independently developed browser extension designed to assist staff members of the 7th Cavalry Gaming Regiment with administrative tasks, communication, and personnel management.

This privacy policy explains how the extension accesses, processes, and stores information.

## 2. Information Accessed

To provide its functionality, the extension may access the following information on the 7Cav website:

- Forum usernames and staff identities.
- Staff positions and organisational assignments.
- Relevant direct-message conversation details.
- User-provided information entered into response templates.

This information is used to generate personalised administrative responses and support staff workflows.

## 3. Data Storage

The extension stores a user's preferred HQ signature locally in their browser using the 7Cav website's local storage.

This information is not uploaded to an external database or shared with the developer.

## 4. Data Sharing

The extension does not sell, transmit, or share user information with third-party services.

Information processed by the extension remains within the user's browser and the 7Cav website.

The extension does not automatically send messages. Users must review and manually submit their responses.

## 5. Data Security

The extension operates on the 7Cav website and uses locally bundled JavaScript to provide its functionality.

It does not require users to provide their passwords or authentication credentials to the extension.

## 6. Future Updates

Additional administrative features may be introduced in future versions. This privacy policy will be updated if new features change how user information is accessed, processed, stored, or shared.

## 7. Disclaimer

The 7Cav Staff Toolkit is an independently developed utility and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by the 7th Cavalry Gaming Regiment.
